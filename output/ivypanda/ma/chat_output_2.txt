Language: Your essay effectively introduces and explains various interactive tools, but the language could be refined for greater clarity and engagement. Here are some key observations and suggestions:

**Strengths:**
- Clear structure with bullet points and explanations
- Good use of technical terms when describing tools
- Logical flow from introduction to conclusion

**Areas for Improvement:**
1. **Tone Consistency:** The language shifts between formal ("It is important realize") and conversational ("After an experience with Prezi"). Maintain a consistent professional tone throughout.

2. **Sentence Structure:** Some sentences are overly long and complex. For example: "The web-based resources and applications presented in this presentation will give the travel agency an opportunity..." could be simplified to "These web-based tools help travel agencies showcase..."

3. **Word Choice:** Replace vague phrases like "a variety of" with more specific language when possible (e.g., "multiple" or "several").

4. **Active Voice:** Use more active constructions. Instead of "The videos can be customized," try "Consultants can customize videos."

5. **Transition Phrases:** Add smoother transitions between sections (e.g., "Moving to our next tool..." instead of abrupt topic shifts).

**Quick Fixes:**
- Remove redundant phrases ("in this modern day and age")
- Eliminate filler words ("there is," "there are")
- Check subject-verb agreement ("allows to create" → "allows users to create")

The content is strong, but polishing the language would make it more compelling and professional. Focus on concise, active sentences and consistent terminology.
