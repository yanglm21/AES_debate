**CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:**

4. **Originality**: Reliance on a single source (Overy, 1997) limits perspective. Incorporating additional scholarly sources would lend credibility and nuance.

4. **Repetition**: Phrases like "upper hand" and "brutally" are overused. Vary vocabulary (e.g., "advantage," "ruthlessly").

1. **Thesis Statement**: The introduction lacks a clear thesis. Specify the core argument upfront (e.g., "Germany lost due to strategic overreach, industrial inferiority, and Allied technological innovation").
