[[72]]: The essay manages to express the importance and usefulness of computers in today's society. However, the language could be more formal for a better presentation to the intended audience.
------
CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay content is relatively focused with valid examples cited. However, the author should pay attention to backing up the value of the computer with more compelling arguments or documented studies, maybe showing negative aspects as well for a balanced view. The benefits of the computer seem to revolve around convenience aspects without delving into the transformative impact on education, technology, and global communication, among others.

LANGUAGE IMPROVEMENTS:
There are several colloquial expressions and incorrect grammar, for instance, "Have you ever forgot" should be "Have you ever forgotten". The author should aim for more formal language suitable for a newspaper. For a stronger impact, phrases such as "I just think that that is a waste of time" could be reworded to be more persuasive, for instance, "it's inefficient and time-consuming in comparison to online searches".

STRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay structure needs some improvement—the thesis is clear, but the argument development could be more structured. The transition between ideas can be smoother, making the essay flow better. The conclusion can be further strengthened by summarizing the main points instead of introducing a new claim.------
