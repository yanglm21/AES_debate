[[70]]: The essay effectively describes the positive effect of computers on people but lacks a degree of coherence and development of ideas; the points are also somewhat too loose and casual for a more formal piece.
------
CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:
The author should provide more specific examples to illustrate the impacts of computers. Current illustrations seem vague and lack depth (ex - "Without computers we would not have some of the knowledge we have"). The writer should also provide a more balanced view, possibly considering negative impacts of computer use, and how these can be addressed or mitigated. This would give a more nuanced argument. 

LANGUAGE IMPROVEMENTS:
The author should improve the tone of the essay to be more formal and academic. The excessive use of first-person narrative and colloquial expressions such as "that's a part of being a kid" or "they would just stomp our feet and clap our hands to make music" detracts from the professionalism of the essay. Grammar improvement is also needed - the phrase "computers have positive affects" for instance, should read "computers have positive effects". 

STRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay could benefit from an improved organization. The classification of the main points into paragraphs does not necessarily follow a logical pattern. The author drifts between education, entertainment, and general impact without clear transitions. Consider implementing clear transitions sentences and perhaps adopting a more classical structure such as: an introduction, three paragraphs dedicated to each main point with cited examples, and a strong conclusion to tie the essay together.
