[[60]]: The essay makes a good attempt at expressing the importance of computers but lacks depth and coherence in the argument and needs substantial improvement in language use and structure.
------
CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:
The writer should strengthen and elaborate on his arguments. For instance, when talking about how computers are a valuable learning resource, it would be more impressive to mention specific academic sites, online courses, or educational programs. The assertion that computers are the best way to keep in touch with friends lacks some nuance - it could be balanced by recognising that other methods also have their merits. Also, the reference to the economic recession seems out of place and needs more context or should be removed entirely.

LANGUAGE IMPROVEMENTS:
Grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors are scattered throughout, harming the overall readability. For example, "their" should be "they're" in several spots, "acession" should be "recession," and "schoold" should be "schools." The wording can often be informal and casual, which is not suitable for an academic essay. Also, phrases like "thing about!" and “But computers do so you can compare your information” are awkwardly phrased, and the intended meaning is unclear.

STRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
The structure could be significantly improved. The main body is rather disorganized, and each paragraph does not clearly identify its primary point. A better approach would be separate paragraphs for each main benefit of computer use (learning, communication, affordability, etc.) with supporting details and examples in each one. The conclusion also abruptly summarizes the preceding points without effectively closing out the discussion.------
