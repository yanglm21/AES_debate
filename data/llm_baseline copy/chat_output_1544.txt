[[55]]: The essay offers a valid perspective on the excessive use of computers but lacks adequate examples and supporting details, and has significant grammatical and formatting errors. It also lacks a clear flow between thoughts.
------
CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay would benefit from more varied examples to support your claims about the negative impacts of computer use. For example, explaining more about the physical or psychological consequences of spending all day in front of the screen or providing statistical data that supports your argument. Implying that excessive computer use directly leads to obesity is too simplistic a connection; this claim needs to be substantiated or presented in a more nuanced manner.

LANGUAGE IMPROVEMENTS:
Your tone is conversational, but it slips into overly colloquial language at times, detracting from the overall argument. Statements like "A lot of humans sit on the computer and eat so over the time they eventually get fat" could be made more persuasive and less informal, for example, “Excessive computer use, often coupled with poor eating habits can result in weight gain over time”. Attention to capitalization rules and the @CAPS1 error need to be fixed. Furthermore, ensure your pronouns match the nouns they refer to for better clarity and precision.




STRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay jumps quickly to different points without a clear connection or transition between them. Use linking words or phrases for a smoother transition. The introduction could be more engaging if it started with a provocative question or a startling fact about computer usage. Also, include a clear thesis statement in the introduction, outlining your main points. Organize your body paragraphs more effectively around one single idea each, developing it fully before moving to the next one. Lastly, summarize your main points more effectively in the conclusion, tying them back to your thesis statement.------
