CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay should explore the topics more thoroughly. Instead of just asserting that computers provide information about other locations, or they aid in homework, offer specific examples or even personal experiences. You also introduced the concept of connecting with distant family and friends quite abruptly - a little context or explanation would work better here. Moreover, you may want to point out some potential drawbacks of computer use to provide a balanced viewpoint.

LANGUAGE IMPROVEMENTS:
There are several spelling and grammar errors throughout the essay, such as "computers does have a positive effect", "Computes help us by giving information about things in othe contries", and "They might start doing something else or get answers for something they can do without the computer." Correct grammar would be "computers do have a positive effect", "Computers help us by providing information about things in other countries" and "They might start doing something else or find answers for something they can do without the computer."

The structuring of arguments within the essay could be improved. Dedicate separate paragraphs to each idea, i.e., one dealing with computers' role in providing information, another discussing their aid in academics, and so on. Also, the conclusion could be stronger by restating the overall argument and providing a final thought.
