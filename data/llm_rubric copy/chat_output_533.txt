[[68]]: The essay presents a good argument about the positive impacts of computers, but lacks depth in some parts, making the argument less solid. Some phrases need editing to improve the language used.
------******
CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:
The writer should try to provide a wider array of arguments. They could mention how computers contribute to medical research, enhance global communication facilitate remote working etc. Also, arguments around the potential negatives of computers (and rebuttals to these) would add to the balance of the essay.

LANGUAGE IMPROVEMENTS:
Editing is needed to improve the professional tone  of the language in the essay. Phrases like "Computers will save me from boredom" could be improved to "Computers provide endless sources of information and entertainment." Instead of using the word "addicted", try "highly dependent". Avoid using informal phrases, like "talking to my friends on Facebook" when a more formal tone would be more appropriate, like "communicating with my peers through social media platforms".

STRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay lacks a proper introduction and conclusion. It would reinforce the argument to start with a clear thesis statement highlighting the main points that will be covered in the essay, and to conclude by summarizing the arguments and restating the thesis. Moreover, the essay would be enhanced by the use of clear paragraphing with each paragraph dedicated to a specific point related to the main argument.
