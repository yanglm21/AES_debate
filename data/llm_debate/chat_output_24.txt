- **Transitional Phrases**: Use transitional phrases to introduce statistics and anecdotes smoothly. For example, "Consider this," or "Interestingly, research shows that..." can lead into a statistic, while "For instance," or "Take the story of..." can introduce an anecdote.

- **Concession and Refutation**: Use a structure where you first concede a point briefly, then refute it with stronger evidence or examples. This can be done in a single paragraph to maintain focus and coherence.
- **Logical Flow**: Ensure that each counterargument naturally follows from the previous point, creating a logical progression. For example, after discussing the benefits of hand-eye coordination, you might segue into addressing concerns about physical activity.
