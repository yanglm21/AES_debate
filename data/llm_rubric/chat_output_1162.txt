CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay would benefit from broader examples instead of just personal experiences. Currently, the arguments revolve around personal anecdotes about maintaining relationships and accessing school assignments. A wider range of points, such as computer usage in professional fields, technological advancements leading to new job opportunities, breakthroughs in medical science etc., would solidify the argument. Furthermore, try to address some of the critics' concerns like excessive computer usage leading to physical or mental health issues or the decline of face-to-face social interaction, and either refute them or suggest solutions to those issues. 

LANGUAGE IMPROVEMENTS:
Pay attention to capitalization and the rules of grammar regarding the use of periods and conjunctions. Erroneous usage of capital letters at the beginning of words and inconsistent punctuation disrupt usage occur frequently through the essay. Additionally, spelling corrections (e.g. 'benifical' to 'beneficial', 'cloths' to 'clothes') should be made. The language could be made more polished and sophisticated by expanding vocabulary and avoiding colloquial phrases.

STRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay follows the typical structure of an argumentative essay but the transitions between points are haphazard. Use transition phrases or words to ensure a smooth flow between paragraphs. Ensure that each paragraph has a clear topic sentence that summarizes the main point of the paragraph. The conclusion could be stronger and more summarising, restating the thesis and tying up the main points made in the essay together, perhaps even offering a vision of the future where computer usage is ubiquitous.
