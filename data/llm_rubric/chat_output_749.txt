CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay's arguments would be more persuasive with stronger evidence and deeper analysis. LANGUAGE IMPROVEMENTS:
Using more sophisticated vocabulary and varying sentence structure could greatly improve the tone of the essay. Additionally, strive for a more formal and less conversational tone by avoiding questions and exclamatory statements. STRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay could benefit from a more dynamic introduction to immediately engage the reader. Consider opening with a compelling fact or statistic about computer use instead of a rhetorical question. Also, each paragraph should be more coherently connected to the next, progressing logically from point to point. A clear conclusion that succinctly restates the main arguments and leaves the reader with a lasting impression would also enhance the essay's effectiveness.
