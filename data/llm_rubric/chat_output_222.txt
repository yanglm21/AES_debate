CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:
The points of argument can be developed further to provide a more in-depth analysis. For instance, the effects of computers on communication can be elaborated more, such as explaining the impact of instant and constant connectivity. Similarly, the argument about using computers for research and writing can include recent developments in online education and digital resources that aid learning.

LANGUAGE IMPROVEMENTS:
The writing seems casual and informal, less suited to an essay format. Many sentences are imprecise or are grammatically incorrect. A more formal and polished language style would enhance the essay's persuasiveness. For instance, 'exersise' should be 'exercise', 'across town' could be rephrased to 'within the city', 'proud' should be 'provide', 'a check for mistakes' could be 'a grammar check' etc. Furthermore, the repeated use of '@' symbols is distracting and unprofessional. 

STRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
The essay could benefit from a stronger organizational structure. The introduction does not effectively outline the argument and the conclusion could be stronger in summarising the points made. Paragraph breaks should be used more effectively to separate different ideas or sections of the essay, improving readability. Finally, the paragraphs should ideally follow a 'point-evidence' model, where each point is followed by evidence or examples validating it.
