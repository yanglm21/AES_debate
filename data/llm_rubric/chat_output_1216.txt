[[68]]: The essay provides a clear response to the question, highlighting the positive effects of computers usage on society. However, the quality of arguments, grammatical errors, and lack of an introduction and a persuasive conclusion undermines the strength of the essay.
------******
CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS:
The writer needs to deepen their arguments with more impactful and concrete examples. For instance, the point about learning about faraway places can be linked to educational benefits and cultural awareness. The point about staying in touch with friends and family can be bolstered by mentioning situations like the pandemic where online communication has been vital. The writer could also address the counterarguments about exercising, enjoying nature, and face-to-face interactions by providing mitigating strategies or highlighting how technology also supports these activities.

LANGUAGE IMPROVEMENTS:
Improper usage of words, spelling mistakes, and lacking punctuation impairs the fluidity of the text. For instance, "convinient" should be "convenient", "it keeps drippy" should be "it keeps dripping" and "buisnesmen" and "buisnes" should be "businessmen" and "business". The use of contractions ("dont", "you'll") should be avoided in formal writing. The possessive pronoun "your" should not be used in place of "you're". 

STRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
An introduction that presents the topic, and what is to follow in the rest of the essay would enhance the overall structure and give the reader a clear sense of the argument being presented. The body of the essay has to be more paragraphed, preferably each point in its own paragraph, followed by a distinct conclusion summarizing the argument and closing with a call to action. Additionally, using good transition words to connect each paragraph would enhance the flow of the essay and make the arguments more persuasive.
